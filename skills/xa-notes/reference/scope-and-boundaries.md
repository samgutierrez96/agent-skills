# APPENDIX B — What an XA can and cannot discuss

Source: XA Boundaries deck. Load this when a note needs to reflect that something
was declined or redirected rather than answered.

**General principle:** an XA can always probe for facts and for the caller's
desired outcome. When in doubt, seek guidance — customers will wait for the right
answer. Any counsel, representation, or negotiation goes to the appropriate
licensee.

## Acquisition — direct seller

**Can discuss:** how Opendoor works, next steps and the Opendoor Key app,
timelines, estimated closing costs, title company, service fee, late checkout and
digital checkout, Final Offer readiness and timeline, denials and walks, appliance
addendum, updating the close date or late checkout, updating names on the contract
before signing, transaction progress, title and escrow help.

**Cannot discuss:** Final Offer details or negotiations, credits and subsidies,
diligence visit results or repairs, signing addenda or contracts, updating names
on the contract after signing, Opendoor Select walks.

## Acquisition — represented seller

Direct back to their agent for anything specific. High-level process is fine — how
Opendoor works, general timelines. Not their Final Offer details, negotiations,
credits, diligence results, or a direct line to Opendoor's agent.

## Acquisition — seller's agent

**Can discuss:** the Agent Access program, referral process, representation, Final
Offer readiness and timeline, denials and walks, appliance addendum, updating the
close date or late checkout, updating names before signing.

**Cannot discuss:** Final Offer details or negotiations, credits, diligence
results, signing addenda, updating names after signing, Opendoor Select walks, or
how the agent gets paid.

## Resale — represented buyer

Direct back to their agent.

**Can help with:** home access for listed, in contract, and closed homes, home
issues and security, listed home information, seller's disclosure with the agent
copied, active offer check, financing options, how their agent submits an offer,
response issues, transaction concerns, a general Opendoor Checkout overview,
closed home issue escalations. A previous owner of a closed Cash Now, More Later
home is a **buyer** for access: go through access guidelines and **do not
provide the lockbox code**.

**Cannot share:** details on another buyer's offer, a seller's disclosure without
the agent copied, negotiation details, or any decision for a home in contract.

## Resale — buyer's agent

**Can help with:** access, home issues, listed home information, past contract
fallthroughs, active offer check, commission and buyer agent compensation
information, seller's disclosures, the listing packet, how to submit an offer and
the agent offer portal, financing options, response issues, coordinating document
signing, transaction concerns, closed home issue escalations.

**Cannot:** negotiate price, repairs, closing costs, financing, title, or the
close of escrow date; sign addenda; approve credits; or make any decision for a
home in contract.

## Non-disclosure states

Sales price cannot be shared in: Alaska, Idaho, Kansas, Louisiana, Mississippi,
Missouri, Montana, New Mexico, North Dakota, Texas, Utah, Wyoming.

Missouri, Texas, and Utah are Opendoor internal policy rather than state law.
