# APPENDIX A — Standing answers

Facts an XA gives on a call. These belong in Solution Offered as advice given,
never as escalations.

## Contacts

| Need | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Trust and safety | trust@opendoor.com |
| Prorated tax requests arriving from other teams | support@opendoor.com |

Give the contact even when an escalation is also noted. The two are not
alternatives.

## Showings and listings
- The home must be vacant by close of escrow.
- Utilities must stay on for 3 or more days past the move out day, not the close
  of escrow date.

## Prorated taxes

At closing, buyer and seller split the tax bill based on days of ownership. If the
current tax bill was higher than the figure used at closing, the buyer may be owed
reimbursement.

Refunds are **always issued by check**, unless there is an extenuating
circumstance such as a wrong address, wrong amount, or a typo. Wire transfers only
happen with Associate Broker approval.

The full ticketing workflow is in `reference/process-detail.md`.
