# Partnership inquiry

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The full playbook remains authoritative: [Per-type recipes](../SKILL.md#per-type-recipes).
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Partnership inquiry:** Separate agent/brokerage partnership from non-agent
  partnership. For agent referral, bonus-count, or missing-transaction questions,
  first read [Agent representation and transaction-count questions](../SKILL.md#agent-representation-and-transaction-count-questions--october-27-2026).
  Representation is required; do not promise referral-only payment or a
  self-service tracker. Review the email-linked Customer history for count
  requests and collect missing property addresses for backend review.
  For current bonus terms, also read
  [Agent bonus program scope](../SKILL.md#agent-bonus-program-scope--verified-november-19-2026).
  Targeted campaign rates are not universal; verify the caller's applicable
  scope and eligibility rather than using an old flyer or generic first-deal rate.
  Agent acquisition purchase-agreement/signature questions use
  [Agent acquisition contract execution](../SKILL.md#agent-acquisition-contract-execution--november-19-2026):
  dashboard setup and seller e-signature, not printed wet-signed contracts by
  email. Keep this separate from buyer/resale offer submission and listing
  agreement intake; no unrequested contract action or completed-signature claim.
  General agent partnership information goes to `https://www.opendoor.com/agents`
  (not `/w/agents`, which is the offer-request flow). Non-agent categories are
  Online Real Estate and Homebuilder; Homebuilder information is at
  `https://www.opendoor.com/homebuilder`, and the partnerships contact is
  `partnerships@opendoor.com`. Do not promise terms for a general strategic or
  investment inquiry; ask for team/business details and offer internal escalation.
