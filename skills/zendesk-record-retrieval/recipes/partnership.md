# Partnership inquiry

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The full playbook remains authoritative: [Per-type recipes](../SKILL.md#per-type-recipes).
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Partnership inquiry:** Separate agent/brokerage partnership from non-agent
  partnership. Agent partnership information goes to `https://www.opendoor.com/agents`
  (not `/w/agents`, which is the offer-request flow). Non-agent categories are
  Online Real Estate and Homebuilder; Homebuilder information is at
  `https://www.opendoor.com/homebuilder`, and the partnerships contact is
  `partnerships@opendoor.com`. Do not promise terms for a general strategic or
  investment inquiry; ask for team/business details and offer internal escalation.
