# Remove one property from an account

Apply AGENTS.md and read SKILL.md → [Privacy and deletion requests](../SKILL.md#privacy-and-deletion-requests)
for the scope boundary, not as authority to delete the whole account. Reuse the
verified ticket/current conversation; [same-ticket follow-up](same-ticket-follow-up.md)
applies when checking newer messages or draft completeness.

Identify what the customer actually wants removed and where it appears. Removing
one property from a profile is not automatically customer-profile deletion,
public-directory/photo removal, cancellation or contact suppression. A missing
ticket property field is not proof that a property is absent from the directory.

If the supported removal process is missing, uncertain or stale, use the shared
kb-answer-for-callers skill to inspect current owner guidance. Do not invent a
self-service control, deletion procedure or permission to edit the record. One
focused scope clarification (for example, the account screen or a redacted
screenshot when genuinely needed) is preferable to an unsupported process claim.
Do not ask again when the existing evidence already identifies the needed screen
and next action. Keep a separate unresolved appointment/offer concern intact.

Only resolve customer/property records when a required association or proposed
record-specific fact needs them; keep applicable identity and recent-resolution
gates. No unrelated stage/assessment/property ladder for a process-only answer.
Return both boxes, distinguish the requested action from verified completion,
and keep the ticket open for genuinely missing information. This card supplies
no new business process and authorizes no deletion, merge, edit, send or post.
