# Same-ticket follow-up

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The full playbook remains authoritative: [Per-type recipes](../SKILL.md#per-type-recipes).
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Same-ticket follow-up:** reuse facts and schemas already in the chat. Do not
  restart customer/property lookups unless the new reply needs a missing fact.
- **Draft completeness review:** questions such as “Did you catch everything?” or
  “Am I missing something?” review the selected draft, not just the newest inbound.
  Check the underlying customer ask, prior support question and any later resolution
  in retained evidence. A name correction or acknowledgment does not resolve a
  separate inquiry. If the needed exchange is missing, read only the matching
  requester thread; do not rerun the full retrieval chain or reload known rules.
  Return both complete draft boxes in the same response, corrected where needed,
  followed by a brief explanation of what changed (or why no change was needed).
  Do not stop at an admission or require another prompt for the corrected drafts.
  An explicit explanation-only/no-edit request keeps the draft unchanged. Preserve
  no-reply dispositions, safety gates and unknowns; do not invent a new question,
  resolution or action. This review never authorizes sending, posting or changes
  to customer records or reusable instructions.
