# Seller asks whether Opendoor can conduct the assessment

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The full playbook remains authoritative: [Sellers with an outstanding assessment](../SKILL.md#sellers-with-an-outstanding-assessment).
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Seller asks whether Opendoor can conduct the assessment:** say that Opendoor
  can schedule an assessment with a licensed professional through Showami to take
  the required photos, or the seller can complete the assessment through the
  **Opendoor Key App**. Ask for the property address and preferred date/time.
  If the home will be vacant and the seller will not be present, ask for the
  access code. Do not say that Opendoor directly conducts the assessment.
