# Privacy objection, portal failure or deletion follow-up

Apply AGENTS.md and read SKILL.md → [Privacy and deletion requests](../SKILL.md#privacy-and-deletion-requests)
plus the required applicable FAQ and matching Privacy thread before drafting.
Reuse already inspected current guidance; retrieve missing/new case evidence only.

Read the relevant prior response, customer's objection and any later Privacy
instruction together. Portal failure alone proves neither a technical defect nor
state eligibility. Do not infer a current eligible-state list from an old case,
repeat failed portal instructions mechanically or invent troubleshooting.
The approved objection responses are conditional, not two mandatory initial
messages. Honor matching-case no-further-response instructions. Unresolved
request-type/eligibility questions go to Privacy for research; promise no outcome.

If escalation is warranted, follow the existing Privacy Request Escalations
workflow/form and [action handoffs](../guides/action-handoffs.md), not a regular
channel-message draft. Prepare only within the operator's authorization; explicit
draft-only permits copyable recommendations, not Slack drafts or form mutation.
Keep Submit untouched. Verified recipients/links/options must not be invented.
A saved draft/prepared form is not a submitted escalation or completed deletion.

Return both boxes: use a supported hold/no-response disposition when required,
with the actual pending next action and evidence gap in the note/appendix.
Do not research unrelated property status solely to fill that note.
