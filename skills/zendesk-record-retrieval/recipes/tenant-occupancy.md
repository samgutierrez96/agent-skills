# Seller policy inquiry about existing tenants

Apply AGENTS.md's Business intent, evidence and conflicting guidance gate.
This is a seller-policy route, not the rental-scam route merely because the
customer mentions tenants, leases or rental income. A prospective renter or
separate suspected scam still uses [rental](rental.md).

1. Verify the ticket, requester, channel and actual question from sufficient
   Zendesk evidence; use current comments when a latest-reply check requires them.
   Do not Glean an exact ticket already returned by Zendesk MCP.
2. Use the shared kb-answer-for-callers skill for current tenant-occupancy/vacancy
   policy. Start with a focused tenant-occupied/vacancy-at-closing search, inspect
   owner-maintained Help Center/KB or applicable #xa-announcements content, and
   follow its linked primary guidance when needed. Read scope and exceptions.
   Historical customer replies are leads, not policy authority on their own.
   Do not repeat equivalent searches after a sufficient current source is read.
3. Keep the reply limited to supported high-level eligibility/process guidance.
   No OpsHub, property, assessment, offer or browser lookup unless a separate
   record-specific question needs it. Do not verify a portfolio's eligibility
   or promise tenants can stay/move out from general wording alone.
4. If the applicable rule remains unverified/conflicting, state the exact gap
   internally and ask only a clarification needed to choose the next action;
   do not replace uncertainty with a confident lease/vacancy assertion.

Return both complete boxes. This card changes routing/source selection only;
it does not establish a new vacancy rule, legal advice or authority to act.
