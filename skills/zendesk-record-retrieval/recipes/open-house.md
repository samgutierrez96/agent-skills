# Open-house wording on an Opendoor listing

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The full playbook remains authoritative: [Public listings](../SKILL.md#public-listings).
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Open-house wording on an Opendoor listing:** Opendoor does not host open houses.
  Treat the request as a viewing request: explain that viewings are scheduled only
  during the property's available viewing window, then direct the customer to the
  Opendoor app or (888) 352-7075 to confirm or schedule an available time. Do not
  confirm, repeat, or invent open-house times.
