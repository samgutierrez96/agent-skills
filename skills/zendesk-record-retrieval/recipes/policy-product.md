# Policy/product question

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The focused full-playbook rule is [Policy and product questions](../SKILL.md#policy-and-product-questions).
Read it with `python3 skills/zendesk-record-retrieval/scripts/read_playbook.py
--section "Policy and product questions"`, not the entire Per-type recipes
collection. This is the routing procedure; use current governing business evidence
for the actual answer. A separate record-specific ask still gets its matching card.
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Policy/product question:** use Zendesk MCP for the exact ticket, or reuse
  sufficient supplied Zendesk evidence. Use Glean/shared kb-answer-for-callers
  for current Help Center, owner-maintained policy or applicable announcements,
  not a duplicate exact-ticket read. Do not open OpsHub unless separate record
  facts are needed; current-comment/freshness checks still apply.
- **Existing tenants/vacancy at closing:** use [tenant occupancy](tenant-occupancy.md)
  directly. Inspect current source content and stop on sufficient evidence;
  historical customer sends alone are not policy authority. Do not classify
  this seller inquiry as a rental scam.
- **Seller closing-date availability:** read the full playbook's Closing bullet
  before quoting a minimum/window. Distinguish affected new/refreshed year-end
  offers (January 2027 default) from already-active offers/existing contracts;
  verify current options. Earlier-2026 exception review is not an approved date
  or a universal quoted fee/price adjustment. Retain the scoped forced-January
  extended-fee safeguard, not a blanket waiver.
- **Home Loans / mortgage licensing or lender referral:** read the full
  playbook's named-listing seller-finance recipe under “Per-type recipes” and
  check [ODHL Disclosures and Licenses](https://www.opendoor.com/homeloans/licenses).
  Distinguish licensed jurisdictions from current product availability and
  borrower eligibility; do not use the former July 2026 market list for routing.
  The licensing page does not establish Lower.com coverage or replace the
  separately documented application steps. For applications, contacts, fees or
  incentives, also read the adjacent "Opendoor Home Loans application start /
  incomplete application" guidance: opendoor.com/mortgage, 947-600-5891 and
  mortgage@opendoor.com. The October 30 XA update also supports these contacts
  for general help with closed loans and provides a preferred booking link in
  the full playbook. Distinguish this team-help referral from the actual loan
  servicer/payment destination; do not promise an officer or response time.
