# Unrelated service request or wrong business

Apply AGENTS.md and the packaged entry contract. Use only when the newest customer
message clearly asks for an unrelated service, such as hiring a bookkeeper for a
different business. A stale listing subject does not turn that into a home inquiry.

1. Verify ticket/requester/channel and newest customer-written intent. Read comments
   when needed to distinguish the new ask from old quoted or automated content.
2. For a clear wrong-business service request with no separate Opendoor support,
   property, privacy or partnership ask, explain concisely that this is Opendoor
   Support for buying or selling homes and that the requested service is not
   provided. No customer/property/trust/offer lookup is needed for that scope-only
   answer; do not imply that a customer record or prior resolution was checked.
3. An unsolicited vendor pitch or mass marketing message uses [vendor SMS](vendor-sms.md)
   or [unsolicited email](unsolicited-email.md), including their lookup/disposition
   rules. A real partnership inquiry uses [partnership](partnership.md). Rental,
   privacy, legal and separate support asks keep their own routes. If ambiguous,
   ask what assistance they need rather than silently marking it spam.

Stop once the newest intent supports this answer. Do not search public listings,
load buyer-interest recipes, or research an unrelated business. Customer reply
first, short factual internal note second, each in its own non-empty copy box.
Use channel-appropriate voice; no unverified action or resolution claims.
