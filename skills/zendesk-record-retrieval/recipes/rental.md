# Rental wording

Apply the packaged entry contract and project rules before this recipe.
The full playbook remains authoritative: [Rental scams](../SKILL.md#rental-scams).
For assessment/photo/app/walkthrough, stage-sensitive, ambiguous, or multi-record asks,
read the matching full-playbook section before drafting; this card is the first lookup path.

- **Rental wording:** use the scam protocol. Add OpsHub for access, codes,
  move-in, prior contact, belongings, or other record-specific history. If the
  customer already has an internal rental-scam incident report and the new
  ticket asks about a callback, property access, or retrieving belongings,
  search the customer's OpsHub thread/activity and Slack for the exact property
  and incident follow-up before drafting. Do not repeat the generic scam
  explanation when the record already establishes it; draft around the latest
  operational status and the next safe handoff. Do not imply belongings remain in
  the home. Include the victim's verified phone and email in the internal report
  so Trust & Safety can contact them directly about scheduling.
